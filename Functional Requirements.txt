================================================================================
FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Fundamentals Data Collection Pipeline (LLM-based, multi-sector)
================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
The pipeline ingests financial documents published by public companies across
all sectors (banks, insurance, industrials, technology, utilities, REITs,
energy, healthcare, etc.) and extracts every numeric fundamental line item
together with the metadata required to interpret it correctly (period type,
period end date, reporting currency, scale, consolidation level, restatement
flag, segment, and source location). Extracted data must satisfy the
mathematical roll-up identities defined in the relationship hierarchy.

================================================================================
1. DOCUMENT INGESTION
================================================================================

FR-1.1  The system MUST accept the following document formats:
        - SEC 10-K (annual report, PDF + HTML)
        - SEC 10-Q (quarterly report, PDF + HTML)
        - SEC 8-K and earnings releases / press releases (PDF, HTML)
        - Earnings supplemental schedules and financial supplements (PDF, XLSX)
        - Investor presentations / earnings decks (PDF, PPTX exported as PDF)
        - XBRL / iXBRL instance documents (XML / .xbrl)
        - Foreign filings: 20-F, 40-F, 6-K, annual reports for non-US issuers
          (PDF, HTML)
        - Proxy statements (DEF 14A) when they contain financial summaries

FR-1.2  The system MUST accept documents via:
        - Bulk upload (S3 / object store prefix)
        - SEC EDGAR scheduled crawl (CIK or ticker driven)
        - Manual upload (single document, ad-hoc reprocessing)
        - Webhook / event trigger (new filing notification)

FR-1.3  Each ingested document MUST be assigned a stable document_id and
        persisted in raw form (immutable) before any processing begins.

FR-1.4  The system MUST capture document-level metadata at ingest:
        - issuer (CIK, ticker, LEI, legal name)
        - filing type (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, earnings release, etc.)
        - filing date and period end date
        - fiscal year and fiscal quarter
        - source URL / source system
        - language (default: en)
        - file hash (SHA-256) for deduplication

FR-1.5  The system MUST detect and skip exact duplicates (same hash) and
        flag near-duplicates (same issuer + period + filing type) for review.

================================================================================
2. DOCUMENT PRE-PROCESSING
================================================================================

FR-2.1  The system MUST classify each document by:
        - document type (filing type)
        - issuer industry / sector (GICS or SIC) for taxonomy selection
        - reporting framework (US GAAP, IFRS, statutory, other)

FR-2.2  The system MUST segment the document into logical sections, including:
        - Consolidated Income Statement / Statement of Operations
        - Consolidated Balance Sheet / Statement of Financial Position
        - Consolidated Cash Flow Statement
        - Statement of Changes in Equity
        - Statement of Comprehensive Income
        - Notes to Financial Statements
        - Segment Disclosures
        - MD&A
        - Selected Financial Data / Five-Year Summary

FR-2.3  For PDF input, the system MUST extract per-page rendered images at
        sufficient DPI (>= 200 DPI) for downstream vision-LLM extraction, and
        MUST also extract the underlying text layer when present.

FR-2.4  The system MUST detect tables within each section and pass each
        candidate financial table to extraction with surrounding context
        (table title, column headers, footnotes, units-of-measure note).

================================================================================
3. LINE-ITEM EXTRACTION (CORE)
================================================================================

FR-3.1  The system MUST extract every numeric line item present in every
        primary financial statement. Coverage MUST include, but is not
        limited to:

        Income Statement:
            Revenue / Net Revenue / Total Net Revenues / Net Sales
            Net Interest Income (banks)
            Non-Interest Income (banks)
            Premium Income (insurance)
            Cost of Revenue / Cost of Goods Sold / Cost of Services
            Gross Profit
            Research & Development
            Selling, General & Administrative
            Other Operating Expenses
            Provision for Credit Losses (banks)
            Claims & Benefits Incurred (insurance)
            Total Operating Expenses
            Operating Income / Operating Profit / Income from Operations
            Interest Income (non-bank)
            Interest Expense
            Other Non-Operating Income / (Expense)
            Income Before Income Tax Expense
            Income Tax Expense / (Benefit)
            Net Income from Continuing Operations
            Net Income from Discontinued Operations
            Net Income
            Net Income Attributable to Non-Controlling Interests
            Net Income Attributable to Common Shareholders
            Basic EPS, Diluted EPS
            Basic Weighted-Average Shares, Diluted Weighted-Average Shares
            Dividends per Share

        Balance Sheet:
            Cash and Cash Equivalents
            Short-Term Investments / Marketable Securities
            Accounts Receivable, net
            Allowance for Credit Losses
            Inventories
            Prepaid Expenses
            Total Current Assets
            Property, Plant & Equipment, net
            Goodwill
            Intangible Assets, net
            Right-of-Use Assets
            Investments in Equity Method / Affiliates
            Loans, net (banks)
            Allowance for Loan Losses (banks)
            Trading Assets / Securities (banks)
            Deferred Tax Assets
            Other Assets
            Total Assets
            Accounts Payable
            Accrued Liabilities
            Short-Term Debt / Current Portion of Long-Term Debt
            Deposits (banks)
            Insurance Reserves (insurance)
            Total Current Liabilities
            Long-Term Debt
            Operating Lease Liabilities
            Deferred Tax Liabilities
            Other Liabilities
            Total Liabilities
            Common Stock
            Additional Paid-In Capital
            Retained Earnings
            Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income / (Loss)
            Treasury Stock
            Non-Controlling Interests
            Total Stockholders' Equity
            Total Liabilities and Stockholders' Equity

        Cash Flow Statement:
            Net Income (starting line)
            Depreciation & Amortization
            Stock-Based Compensation
            Deferred Income Taxes
            Changes in Working Capital (with sub-items)
            Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities
            Capital Expenditures / Purchases of PP&E
            Acquisitions, net of cash acquired
            Purchases / Proceeds of Investments
            Net Cash Used in Investing Activities
            Proceeds from / Repayment of Debt
            Issuance / Repurchase of Common Stock
            Dividends Paid
            Net Cash Used in Financing Activities
            Effect of Exchange Rate Changes on Cash
            Net Change in Cash
            Cash at Beginning of Period
            Cash at End of Period

        Per-Share / Other:
            Shares Outstanding (period-end)
            Treasury Shares
            Book Value per Share
            Dividends Declared per Share

FR-3.2  Extraction MUST not be limited to the canonical names above. The
        system MUST capture issuer-specific line item labels verbatim and map
        them to a canonical taxonomy via a configurable label-to-canonical
        dictionary, with confidence score and ability to flag unmapped items
        for human review.

FR-3.3  Each extracted line item MUST carry the following fields:
        - canonical_name              (mapped taxonomy key)
        - reported_label              (verbatim text from the document)
        - value                       (raw numeric value as printed)
        - normalized_value            (value * scale, in base currency units)
        - sign                        (positive / negative as printed,
                                       including parenthetical negatives)
        - currency                    (ISO 4217, e.g. USD, EUR, GBP, JPY)
        - scale                       (units, hundreds, thousands, millions,
                                       billions, percent, basis points)
        - period_type                 (annual, quarterly, year-to-date,
                                       trailing-twelve-months, point-in-time)
        - period_start_date           (for flow items)
        - period_end_date             (for stock items)
        - fiscal_year, fiscal_quarter
        - consolidation_level         (consolidated, parent-only, segment,
                                       geographic, business-line)
        - segment_id                  (when consolidation_level != consolidated)
        - reporting_framework         (US-GAAP, IFRS, statutory, other)
        - restatement_flag            (as-reported, restated, reclassified)
        - audit_status                (audited, reviewed, unaudited)
        - source_document_id
        - source_section              (e.g. "Consolidated Statements of Income")
        - source_page                 (page number in PDF)
        - source_bbox                 (bounding box on the source page)
        - source_table_id             (table identifier within the section)
        - extraction_method           (vision-llm, text-llm, xbrl, ocr)
        - confidence                  (0.0 - 1.0)
        - extracted_at                (UTC timestamp)
        - model_version               (LLM model + prompt version hash)

FR-3.4  The system MUST capture multi-period columns presented in a single
        table (e.g. "Three Months Ended March 31, 2025 / 2024" produces two
        rows per line item, one per period).

FR-3.5  The system MUST distinguish CONSOLIDATED data from SEGMENT data and
        store both. Segment data MUST be linked to its parent consolidated
        view via segment_id and segment_dimension (business / geography /
        product).

FR-3.6  The system MUST detect and correctly handle:
        - Parenthetical negatives:    (1,234)  ->  -1234
        - Dash / em-dash placeholders for zero or N/A
        - Footnote markers:           1,234(a)  -> value 1234, footnote_ref a
        - Restated columns and "as previously reported" columns
        - "n/m" (not meaningful), "*" (rounding), "-" (zero or null)
        - Currency symbols mixed in cells ($, €, £, ¥, ₹)
        - Mixed scales within a single table (e.g. EPS in actuals while the
          rest of the table is in millions)

================================================================================
4. METADATA & SCALING NORMALIZATION
================================================================================

FR-4.1  The system MUST detect the table-level scale declaration from any of:
        "(in millions)", "(in thousands, except per share)", "(US$ millions)",
        "(amounts in INR crore)", "(图片)", and apply it correctly to every
        cell in the table unless overridden by a row-level note (e.g. EPS).

FR-4.2  The system MUST detect and store the reporting currency from:
        - explicit table headers ("US$ millions", "in EUR thousands")
        - issuer's functional / presentation currency from cover page
        - footnote currency disclosures

FR-4.3  Where a document is reported in multiple currencies (e.g. USD primary
        with EUR convenience translation), the system MUST capture both and
        flag which is the primary presentation currency.

FR-4.4  The system MUST resolve relative period labels into absolute dates,
        e.g. "Three Months Ended March 31" -> period_start_date = 2025-01-01,
        period_end_date = 2025-03-31, period_type = quarterly.

FR-4.5  The system MUST distinguish:
        - flow items   (revenue, cash flow)        -> period_start + period_end
        - stock items  (balance sheet, shares o/s) -> period_end (point-in-time)

FR-4.6  Non-calendar fiscal years MUST be handled (e.g. fiscal year ending
        in January for retailers, in June for some non-US issuers).

================================================================================
5. RELATIONSHIP / IDENTITY VALIDATION
================================================================================

FR-5.1  The system MUST maintain a sector-aware Mathematical Relationship
        Hierarchy (see Pipeline Design.md, section "Relationship Hierarchy")
        that defines parent / child roll-up identities for each statement.

FR-5.2  After extraction, every parent-child identity in the hierarchy MUST
        be evaluated. Examples:
        - Net Income = Income Before Tax - Income Tax Expense
        - Total Assets = Total Current Assets + Total Non-Current Assets
        - Total Assets = Total Liabilities + Total Stockholders' Equity
        - Net Change in Cash = CFO + CFI + CFF + FX Effect
        - Gross Profit = Revenue - Cost of Revenue
        - Total Net Revenue (bank) = Net Interest Income + Non-Interest Income
        - Income Before Tax (bank) = Net Revenue - Provision - Non-Interest Exp.

FR-5.3  Identity checks MUST tolerate rounding within +/- the smallest unit
        implied by the table's scale (e.g. +/- $1M when reported in millions),
        configurable per identity.

FR-5.4  Failed identities MUST NOT silently overwrite extracted values.
        Failures MUST produce a validation_issue record with:
        - identity_id, expected_value, computed_value, delta, tolerance,
          contributing line items, and remediation status.

FR-5.5  When a parent line item is missing but all children are present
        (and vice versa), the system MUST mark the missing item as
        "derivable" and store the derived value separately from the extracted
        value, never overwriting an extracted value with a derived one.

FR-5.6  The relationship hierarchy MUST be versioned and editable without
        code changes (declarative YAML / JSON).

================================================================================
6. OUTPUT & STORAGE
================================================================================

FR-6.1  Extracted facts MUST be persisted in a normalized fact store with the
        schema described in FR-3.3, keyed by:
        (issuer_id, canonical_name, period_end_date, period_type,
         consolidation_level, segment_id, restatement_flag).

FR-6.2  The system MUST preserve as-reported values AND normalized values
        side by side. As-reported is the source of truth; normalized values
        are derived (currency scale, sign convention, taxonomy mapping).

FR-6.3  The system MUST support point-in-time queries: "what was reported as
        of date D" must return the values present in the latest filing whose
        filing_date <= D, with restated values accessible separately.

FR-6.4  The system MUST emit machine-readable output in at least:
        - JSON (per-document fact bundle)
        - Parquet (analytical fact table)
        - CSV (human review)

FR-6.5  Every extracted fact MUST be traceable end-to-end: a user MUST be
        able to click a value and see the source page, bounding box, and
        the LLM prompt + response that produced it.

================================================================================
7. HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP REVIEW
================================================================================

FR-7.1  The system MUST surface a review queue for facts where:
        - confidence < configurable threshold (default 0.85)
        - identity validation failed and could not be reconciled
        - canonical mapping is ambiguous or unmapped
        - scale / currency could not be determined
        - the value differs by > X% from the prior period without disclosure

FR-7.2  Reviewers MUST be able to accept, edit, or reject facts. Every edit
        MUST be audit-logged with reviewer_id, timestamp, before/after values,
        and reason code.

FR-7.3  Reviewer corrections MUST be fed back as labeled training data for
        prompt tuning, fine-tuning, and taxonomy mapping improvement.

================================================================================
8. RE-EXTRACTION & RESTATEMENT HANDLING
================================================================================

FR-8.1  When a new filing supersedes an earlier one (10-K restating prior
        10-Qs, or amended 10-K/A), the system MUST:
        - retain the prior extracted facts immutably
        - extract the new filing as a separate version
        - link them via restatement_chain_id
        - flag deltas above tolerance for analyst review

FR-8.2  The system MUST support full re-extraction of any document on demand
        (e.g. after an extraction model upgrade) without losing history.

================================================================================
9. SECURITY, ACCESS & AUDIT
================================================================================

FR-9.1  All access to documents and facts MUST be authenticated and
        role-based (ingest, extract, review, read-only).

FR-9.2  Every read and write of a fact MUST be audit-logged.

FR-9.3  The system MUST not transmit document content to any LLM provider
        not on the approved list, and MUST honor data-residency
        configuration per issuer / per tenant.

================================================================================
10. APIs
================================================================================

FR-10.1 The system MUST expose REST / gRPC endpoints for:
        - submit_document(document, metadata) -> document_id
        - get_document_status(document_id)
        - get_facts(issuer, period, canonical_names[]) -> facts[]
        - get_validation_issues(document_id) -> issues[]
        - submit_review(fact_id, decision, value, reason) -> ack

FR-10.2 The system MUST expose a streaming events feed for downstream
        consumers: fact.created, fact.updated, document.completed,
        validation.failed, review.completed.
